What I do

I review, redesign and automate — in that order.

01
WhatsApp Experience Review
I experience your WhatsApp journey as a real customer and identify where it can be faster, clearer and more consistent. I look for waiting points, repeated questions, missing context and differences across entry points.
02
Journey Redesign
I simplify unnecessary steps and redesign the conversation so every interaction helps the customer move forward.
The goal is not to automate more. The goal is to make the journey better first.
03
AI + Automation
Once the journey is clear, I automate repetitive interactions such as FAQs, offers, qualification, follow-ups and booking steps. Humans stay where they add real value.
